Task understanding before scale

A legal-intelligence program needed to evaluate conflict detection and memo drafting across a master agreement, schedules, regulatory material and revision history. Legal specialists fixed jurisdiction, time and controlling-text versions before clause extraction, conflict analysis, research QA and citation checks. Material disagreement entered blind review and adjudication. This is a sanitized representative delivery structure and does not constitute legal advice.
