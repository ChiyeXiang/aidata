Task understanding before scale

An education model needed to distinguish concept mastery, reasoning errors and incomplete expression rather than mark only final-answer correctness. Subject teachers mapped curriculum standards to a knowledge graph, authored original items and supplied reference solutions, step-level rubrics and misconception patterns. A second teacher solved independently, while evaluation researchers used model pilots and item-response analysis to check discrimination, bias and scoring agreement.
