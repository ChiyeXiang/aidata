Task understanding before scale

A financial-model team needed to test whether the model could reason across multi-year statements, credit policy, sector research and risk clauses without losing evidentiary grounding. We defined the document hierarchy, temporal scope and citation protocol before finance-qualified authors constructed research, valuation, risk and compliance tasks. Independent reviewers assessed conclusions and evidence spans without seeing the original judgment. Figures shown are from a representative sanitized batch; identities, source material and selected volumes have been removed or banded.
